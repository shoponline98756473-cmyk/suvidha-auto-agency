This Privacy Policy explains how M/s Suvidha Auto Agency ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses and protects the information you provide when you visit our website or contact us. By using this website you agree to the practices described below.

Information We Collect

We collect personal information only when you choose to submit it to us through the contact form on our website. This information may include:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • The service you are interested in
  • Any message or details you provide

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not knowingly collect payment card details or government identification numbers through this website.

How We Use Your Information

The information you submit is used solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry or service request
  • Contact you regarding appointments, quotations or follow-up
  • Maintain a basic record of customer communication

No Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with any third parties. Your details are used only by M/s Suvidha Auto Agency for the purposes described in this policy, except where disclosure is required by law.

Data Retention

We keep the information you provide only for as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry and to maintain reasonable business records. You may request that we delete your information by contacting us.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information submitted through our website. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

You may contact us to ask what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to request its deletion. We will respond to reasonable requests within a reasonable time.
