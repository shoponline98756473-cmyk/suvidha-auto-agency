Our Story

Founded in Jaunpur, Built on Trust

M/s Suvidha Auto Agency was founded by Atul Mishra in 2023 as a proprietorship vehicle service and repair workshop in Jaunpur, Uttar Pradesh. What began as a commitment to give local vehicle owners honest, dependable repair work has grown into a full-service workshop trusted by hundreds of customers across the region.

Our workshop at Sadar, Mohammadpur, Baksha, Jaunpur handles routine servicing, engine repairs, tyre and wheel care, electrical work, denting & painting and air-conditioning service. Every job is carried out by experienced technicians using quality parts, so our customers drive away with confidence.
